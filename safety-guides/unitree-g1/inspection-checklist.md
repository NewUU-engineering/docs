# Unitree G1 Inspection Checklist

Print or copy this for every session. The robot isn't powered on until every "before power-on" box is ticked. Any "no" goes to the supervisor first. The body, battery and remote items follow the manual's own checklists (p.26–27). Page numbers refer to the [Unitree G1 User Manual V1.1](https://reliablerobotics.ai/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/G1-User-Manual_compressed.pdf). The procedures and buttons are explained in the [safety guide](README.md).

## Before power-on

**Area and people**
- [ ] Operator and spotter present, and both know that L2 + B is the stop
- [ ] Zone clear: floor flat, dry and clean; robot at least 2 m from obstacles and people (p.11)
- [ ] Frame installed and its wheels locked (p.12), **or** for a seated start: a sturdy chair without wheels, in an approved space
- [ ] Rope, both shoulder buckles and the knot checked
- [ ] Cables routed and taped outside the robot's path

**Body** (robot off, p.26)
- [ ] Body clean, with no cracks, damage or deformation
- [ ] Camera lens clean; nothing blocking the LiDAR on the head
- [ ] Joints, connections and feet checked by eye and by touch
- [ ] All screws tight, especially on the joint connectors and the battery locking knob
- [ ] Cooling fan intake and exhaust not blocked
- [ ] Foot pads not damaged
- [ ] No water, oil, sand or soil inside the robot (p.12)

**Battery** (p.26)
- [ ] Battery connector on the body clean and not deformed
- [ ] Battery shell undamaged (a visibly damaged pack must not be used)
- [ ] Battery charged, clicked in with the buckle in place, and not loose (p.12)

**Remote and code**
- [ ] Remote charged; sticks centred and clean; no sticking buttons (p.26)
- [ ] Label on the back of the remote readable
- [ ] If you're running your own code: it passed the earlier stages and the supervisor approved this stage

## After power-on (p.27)

- [ ] Start-up finished: all joints in zero torque (limp) within about 1 minute (p.16)
- [ ] Remote connected: right DL light on (p.20)
- [ ] Remote and robot battery levels sufficient
- [ ] Cooling fan running normally, with no scratching noise
- [ ] **Stop tested: L2 + B pressed while the robot hangs or sits, and it went into damping**
- [ ] If you're running your own low-level code: Develop mode on (L2 + R2, checked with L2 + A), then back to damping with L2 + B

## Post-operation

- [ ] Shut down while hanging or seated, after L2 + B (p.22)
- [ ] Remote switched off
- [ ] Joints arranged for the next start; battery removed if the robot won't be used soon (p.22)
- [ ] Body wiped with a dry, soft cloth, especially the camera and LiDAR (p.26)
- [ ] Body, joints, feet and hands checked again for new damage
- [ ] Battery charged only once cool; remote charged
- [ ] Session logged: duration, what ran, any falls, incidents or noises
- [ ] Any damage reported; robot tagged "DO NOT USE" until the supervisor clears it

## After a fall (before the next power-on)

- [ ] Battery: no shell damage, swelling, heat or smell
- [ ] Body: no new cracks or deformation; covers in place; screws tight
- [ ] Every joint moves smoothly, with no new noise or play
- [ ] Foot pads, hands, camera and LiDAR intact
- [ ] Supervisor has seen the robot and approved the next power-on
